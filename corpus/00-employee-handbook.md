## 1. Welcome

Harborline Technologies, Inc. (“Harborline,” “the Company,” “we”) builds operations software for mid-market logistics, field service, and wholesale distribution companies. We are a single company with three offices and a distributed workforce:

- **Seattle headquarters** — 418 Occidental Ave S, Suite 600, Seattle, WA 98104
- **Austin office** — 600 Congress Ave, Floor 14, Austin, TX 78701
- **Dublin office** — 2 Grand Canal Square, Dublin 2, D02 A342, Ireland

This handbook introduces the policies that govern day-to-day work. It is not a contract and it does not promise employment for any period of time. Detailed procedures live in the numbered policies listed above. If this handbook and a dedicated policy disagree, the dedicated policy controls for that topic.

People Operations publishes the current versions in HarborHub under **Company > Policies**. Printed copies, PDF extracts, and onboarding packets may lag the HarborHub copy. When in doubt, use the version dated in the document header and confirm with hr@harborline.example.

## 4. Employment classifications

People Operations assigns a classification in HarborHub on or before your start date. Classification drives overtime, PTO accrual, and benefit eligibility.

**Exempt employees** are paid a salary and are not eligible for overtime under the US Fair Labor Standards Act (or the Irish equivalent for Dublin roles that are designated as salaried). Exempt employees still record PTO, holidays, and leaves so staffing plans stay accurate.

**Non-exempt employees** are paid hourly, record all hours worked in HarborHub Time, and receive overtime at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek (US) or as required by Irish law. Working off the clock is prohibited. If a manager asks you to work and the time is not in the system, tell Payroll the same day.

**Full-time** means a regular schedule of 30 or more hours per week. **Part-time** means 20–29 hours. Schedules under 20 hours are used only for interns, students, or written exceptions approved by the Chief People Officer.

Your workweek for overtime and PTO is Sunday 12:00 a.m. through Saturday 11:59 p.m. in the time zone of your assigned office, or Pacific Time if you are a US remote employee with no assigned office.

## 5. Equal employment and accommodations

Harborline is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other status protected by US, state, local, or Irish law.

If you need a reasonable accommodation for a disability, pregnancy, or religious practice, email hr@harborline.example with a short description of the limitation and the job task it affects. People Operations will begin an interactive process, which may include a medical questionnaire sent to your provider and a conversation about alternatives. Do not buy equipment or change your schedule on your own and assume the Company will reimburse it; accommodation purchases go through People Operations and IT together.

Harassment and retaliation are covered in **POL-CON-010**. The reporting paths in that policy apply even if the person involved is a customer, vendor, or contractor.

## 6. Pay, timekeeping, and the workday

Harborline pays US employees semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of the month. If a pay date falls on a weekend or US company holiday, we pay on the preceding business day. Dublin employees are paid monthly on the last Friday of the month.

Direct deposit is required. Update bank details in HarborHub at least five business days before the next pay date. Paper checks are issued only when a bank change misses the cutoff or a payment fails.

The standard US office workday is 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. local time with a 30-minute unpaid meal period for non-exempt employees who work more than five hours. Remote and hybrid employees follow **POL-RMT-003** core hours (10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their local time) unless their manager publishes a team-specific window.

Non-exempt employees must clock out for meal periods of 30 minutes or more. Rest breaks of 10–15 minutes are paid and do not require a clock-out. Managers may not pressure anyone to skip a legally required meal period.

## 7. Personnel records and privacy

Your personnel file lives in HarborHub and in a limited People Operations archive. You may request a copy of your US personnel file once per year, or as provided by state law (California, Illinois, and other states may allow more frequent access). Email hr@harborline.example. We will provide the file within 30 days, or sooner if a state statute requires it.

Do not store official offer letters, performance ratings, or investigation notes in a personal email account or a consumer cloud drive. Those records are Company records.

Employee personal data is **Confidential** under **POL-SEC-005**. Customer data may be **Restricted**. Both classes have handling rules that apply at home, in the office, and on the road.

## 9. Policy updates and exceptions

People Operations may revise any policy with or without advance notice when law, insurance, or business needs change. Material changes are announced in Slack #harbor-people and by email. The effective date in the header is the date the new rule applies.

A manager cannot waive a policy. Exceptions require written approval:

- Compensation, leave, or classification exceptions: Chief People Officer
- Expense or travel exceptions over published caps: VP of Finance (or CFO above $2,500)
- Security exceptions (personal devices, USB, vendor access): Head of Information Security
- Remote-work location or international-work exceptions: Chief People Officer and Head of Information Security together

Approvals live in HarborHub as a case. A Slack message is not an exception.
