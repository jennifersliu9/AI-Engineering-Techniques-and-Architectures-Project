# Paid Time Off Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-PTO-001  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Regular full-time and part-time employees  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-HOL-002, POL-LOA-009, POL-FAM-011, POL-RMT-003

---

## 1. Purpose

Harborline uses a single Paid Time Off (PTO) bank for vacation, personal days, and most planned absences. PTO is how we staff the company without requiring employees to label every day as “vacation” or “personal.” This policy explains who earns PTO, how fast it accrues, how to request it, what happens at year-end, and how PTO interacts with holidays, sick time, and leaves of absence.

PTO is not a substitute for legally protected leave. If you need time away for a serious health condition, bonding with a new child, military service, or a qualifying family reason, start with **POL-LOA-009** or **POL-FAM-011**. Those leaves may run beside PTO; they are not denied because you still have a PTO balance.

## 2. Eligibility

| Status | PTO | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular full-time (30+ hours/week) | Yes | Accrues at the full schedule in Section 3 |
| Regular part-time (20–29 hours/week) | Yes, prorated | Accrual = full-time rate × (scheduled weekly hours ÷ 40) |
| Interns, co-ops, and agency temps | No | They observe company holidays if the office is closed |
| Employees in Ireland | Yes | Company PTO is provided **in addition to** Irish statutory annual leave. Combined time off will not fall below the statutory minimum of 20 working days plus public holidays |

PTO begins accruing on your first day of work. You may use accrued PTO after 30 calendar days of employment, except that People Operations may approve earlier use when an offer letter already promised dates (a pre-planned wedding or a relocation trip). Those dates must appear in HarborHub before your start date.

Employees who transfer from contractor or intern status to regular employment keep their original Harborline start date for tenure bands if the gap between assignments was 30 days or less.

## 3. Accrual schedule (US and Canada-based employees)

Harborline accrues PTO each semi-monthly pay period (24 periods per year). Balances in HarborHub are shown in hours. One PTO day equals 8 hours for full-time employees.

| Completed years of service | Annual grant | Hours per year | Hours per pay period |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hire through 2nd anniversary | 15 days | 120 | 5.00 |
| Day after 2nd anniversary through 6th anniversary | 20 days | 160 | 6.67 |
| Day after 6th anniversary onward | 25 days | 200 | 8.33 |

Tenure changes apply on the first pay period that begins **after** the anniversary date. Example: if your second anniversary is 10 March 2026, the 20-day rate starts on the 16 March 2026 pay period, not on 10 March.

Part-time example: a 24-hour-per-week employee in the first tenure band earns 120 × (24 ÷ 40) = 72 hours per year, or 3.00 hours per pay period.

Harborline does not use a separate “unlimited PTO” program. Managers may not invent a different annual grant for their team. A higher grant in an offer letter is valid only when People Operations put it in the offer and in HarborHub.

## 4. Sick time (US)

US employees also receive a **sick bank** that is separate from PTO:

- Full-time: 10 days (80 hours) granted on 1 January, or on the first day of work if you start mid-year (prorated by remaining months ÷ 12).
- Part-time: prorated on scheduled hours.
- Unused sick time does **not** carry over and is **not** paid out at year-end or at termination.
- Sick time is for your illness, a preventive appointment, or care of a household family member who is ill.

If you are out sick for more than three consecutive workdays, your manager will ask People Operations to send the short-term disability and medical-leave packet. You may be required to provide a fitness-for-duty note before you return. See **POL-LOA-009**.

Some US cities and states (including Seattle, Washington state, Austin/Texas local rules where applicable, California if you work there, and others) mandate sick leave. Harborline’s 10-day sick bank is intended to meet or exceed those mandates. If a local ordinance is more generous, the local rule controls. Email hr@harborline.example if you work from a city that is not Seattle, Austin, or Dublin.

Ireland-based employees follow Irish sick-pay statute plus the company occupational sick-pay scheme described in **POL-LOA-009**. They do not receive the US 10-day sick bank on top of that scheme.

## 5. How to request PTO

1. Open HarborHub > Time Off > Request.
2. Choose **PTO** (not Sick, Holiday, or Leave).
3. Enter the hours. The minimum increment is **1 hour**. Full days are 8 hours for full-time staff.
4. Add a comment if the dates sit next to a holiday, a product launch, or a customer on-site.
5. Submit. HarborHub routes to your manager.

**Notice expectations**

| Length of request | Minimum notice | Approval target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–8 hours | End of the prior workday | 1 business day |
| 2–3 consecutive workdays | 5 business days | 2 business days |
| 4–9 consecutive workdays | 15 calendar days | 5 business days |
| 10 or more consecutive workdays | 30 calendar days | 10 business days |

A late request is not automatically denied. Managers may approve it when coverage exists. A pattern of same-day PTO may be treated as an attendance issue under **POL-CON-010**.

If two people on the same on-call rotation request the same week, the earlier HarborHub timestamp wins unless a documented customer commitment requires the opposite. Seniority is not the tie-breaker.

## 6. Blackout and limited-approval periods

Engineering, Customer Success, and Finance may publish blackout windows when a product launch, year-end close, or major customer cutover needs full coverage. Rules:

- Blackouts are announced at least **60 days** in advance in Slack and by email.
- A blackout may last no longer than 10 consecutive business days unless the Chief People Officer approves a longer window.
- PTO already approved before the announcement stays approved.
- New requests during a blackout need the manager **and** the director of the function.

Company holidays are never blackout days. You cannot be required to work a listed holiday without holiday premium or a substitute day as described in **POL-HOL-002**.

## 7. Carryover, caps, and year-end

You may carry a maximum of **40 hours** of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Hours above 40 as of 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on 31 December are forfeited, except:

- People Operations approved a written carryover exception before 15 December because a business need blocked your time off; or
- A state law (notably California) requires different treatment of vested vacation. California employees should treat accrued PTO as wages. Harborline does not apply the 40-hour cap as a forfeiture in California; instead we may require employees to take time down toward a 1.5× annual-grant cap, which is lawful vacation-cap practice. Email hr@harborline.example if your work state is California, Massachusetts, or Nebraska.

HarborHub shows a projected forfeiture starting 1 October. Managers should not wait until December to talk about unused balances.

Sick time does not carry over. Floating holidays do not carry over (see **POL-HOL-002**).

## 8. Negative balances and advances

With manager and People Operations approval, you may go negative by up to **16 hours** of PTO. The deficit is repaid from future accruals. If you leave Harborline while negative, the deficit is deducted from the final paycheck where state law allows. We will not deduct below minimum wage for non-exempt employees.

PTO advances larger than 16 hours are not available.

## 9. Unplanned absences and no-call / no-show

If you cannot work and did not request PTO in advance:

- Notify your manager by Slack or phone before your scheduled start (or before 10:00 a.m. local if you have no fixed start).
- Enter Sick or PTO in HarborHub the same day.
- Three consecutive workdays without notice is a **no-call / no-show** and may be treated as job abandonment after People Operations attempts contact at your emergency number.

Using PTO does not excuse you from on-call duties unless the on-call shift was also covered.

## 10. Interaction with holidays and leaves

- If a company holiday falls during approved PTO, the holiday is coded **Holiday**, not PTO. You do not lose PTO hours for that day. See **POL-HOL-002**.
- If you start a medical or family leave, stop using PTO for the same days unless you elect to supplement unpaid leave with PTO. People Operations will ask you to make that election in writing.
- Parental leave under **POL-FAM-011** is paid by the Company. You are not required to exhaust PTO first.
- Jury duty and bereavement have their own pay codes and do not draw from PTO for the durations in **POL-LOA-009**.

## 11. Payout at termination

Unused, accrued PTO is paid on the final paycheck at your then-current base rate, except:

- You are terminated for willful misconduct after an investigation documented by People Operations; **and**
- Your work state allows an employer to withhold vacation payout in that situation.

Sick time, floating holidays, and future (not-yet-accrued) PTO are not paid out.

Resignation notice is two weeks for most roles and four weeks for director and above. We may pay PTO during the notice period if we ask you not to work. We may also decline to schedule new PTO during a notice period if customer coverage would fail.

## 12. Recording time honestly

Exempt employees do not record daily hours, but they must record PTO, sick, holiday, and leave codes. Entering “worked” on a day you were out is a conduct issue.

Non-exempt employees must record actual hours. PTO is used to bring a short week up to scheduled hours when you were away. You may not use PTO to create overtime.

## 13. Examples employees actually ask about

**Q. I started on 15 August. How much PTO do I have on 1 December?**  
A. You accrue 5.00 hours each semi-monthly period in year one. From the 16 August period through the 30 November period you will have roughly 8 pay periods × 5.00 = 40 hours, minus anything you already used. HarborHub is the system of record.

**Q. Can I take Friday and the following Monday without using Wednesday–Thursday if those are holidays?**  
A. Yes. Code the holidays as Holiday. PTO is only for the working days you are away.

**Q. My manager said the team uses unlimited PTO.**  
A. That is not a Harborline program. Use this policy. If an offer letter promised a different grant, send it to hr@harborline.example.

**Q. Does unused PTO pay out when I move from US payroll to Irish payroll?**  
A. Yes, we close the US PTO bank and pay it, then start Irish annual-leave tracking. People Operations will schedule the conversion with Payroll.

**Q. I am part-time at 20 hours. Do I still get 10 sick days?**  
A. No. Sick time is prorated: 80 × (20 ÷ 40) = 40 hours for the year if you were employed on 1 January.

## 14. Administration

People Operations owns this policy. Payroll owns the paycheck. HarborHub owns the balance you see. If the three disagree, Payroll investigates and People Operations issues a written correction within two pay periods.

To appeal a denied PTO request, email hr@harborline.example within seven calendar days. Include the request ID and why coverage exists. The Director of People Operations decides appeals.
