Benefits Enrollment Guide

Use this guide with POL-BEN-006 Employee Benefits. The Summary Plan Description and HarborHub election screens control if a dollar amount here is out of date.

1. Three ways coverage can start or change

EventWindowCoverage effectiveIf you miss it

New hires: elect as soon as HarborHub is live. Do not wait until medical “starts” on the first of the next month. Example: start date 12 January 2026 → medical starts 1 February 2026 → election deadline 11 February 2026.

2. Qualifying life events we actually see

  • Marriage, domestic partnership (where the plan allows), divorce, or legal separation
  • Birth, adoption, or placement of a child — 30 days, even if you are on parental leave (POL-FAM-011)
  • Loss of other coverage (spouse’s employer, Medicaid, a parent’s plan at age 26)
  • Change in work hours that changes eligibility (part-time ↔ full-time)
  • A move that takes you in or out of an HMO service area (we rarely offer HMOs; still tell Benefits)
  • Death of a covered dependent

Feeling healthier, wanting a cheaper paycheck deduction in June, or a landlord raising rent are not QLEs. “I forgot during week one” is not a QLE.

3. Step-by-step in HarborHub

  1. Sign in at HarborHub → Benefits → Enroll.
  2. Choose medical (PPO or HDHP+HSA), dental, vision, FSA/HSA, supplemental life, and ESPP.
  3. Upload dependent proof within 30 days of adding a person (marriage certificate, birth certificate, placement letter).
  4. Name life-insurance beneficiaries. If you skip this, the plan’s default order applies.
  5. Set 401(k) percent. Auto-enroll is 3% on day 30; the match is the first 4% (POL-BEN-006).
  6. Submit. Print or save the confirmation PDF. Screenshot the confirmation number in case the session drops.

6. Ireland

Dublin employees enroll in the Irish private health scheme through the local benefits packet, not the US medical screens. PRSA enrollment is separate: Company contributes 5% when you contribute at least 5%. Questions: benefits@harborline.example with subject “Ireland enrollment.”
