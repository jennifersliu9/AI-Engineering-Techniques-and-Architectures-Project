# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-RMT-003  
**Owner:** People Operations (with Workplace and Information Security)  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** All employees  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-PTO-001, POL-EQP-008, POL-SEC-005, POL-EXP-004, POL-ONB-007

## 1. Purpose and design

Harborline is a hybrid company. We keep two US hubs and one EU hub so customers, new hires, and teams that need whiteboards can meet in person. We also hire people who live outside commuting range because the talent we need is not only in Seattle, Austin, and Dublin.

This policy defines work location categories, office-day expectations, core hours, home-office support, travel between home and hub, and the rules for working from another state or country. It does not replace **POL-SEC-005**. A kitchen table is still a Harborline workplace when you are logged in.

## 2. Location categories

People Operations assigns one location category in HarborHub. Your offer letter uses the same words.

| Category | Definition | Office expectation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hub — Seattle** | Primary workplace is the Seattle office. Home address is within 50 miles of 418 Occidental Ave S. | At least **3 days per week** in the office |
| **Hub — Austin** | Primary workplace is the Austin office. Home address is within 50 miles of 600 Congress Ave. | At least **3 days per week** in the office |
| **Hub — Dublin** | Primary workplace is the Dublin office. Home address is within 50 km of Grand Canal Square. | At least **3 days per week** in the office |
| **Remote — US/Canada** | Approved to work from a US or Canadian home office more than 50 miles (or 50 km in Canada) from any hub, or in a role posted as remote. | No regular office days. Optional visits per Section 8 |
| **Remote — Ireland** | Approved to work from an Irish home office outside the Dublin radius. | No regular office days |
| **Temporary remote** | Hub employee approved to work away from the office for 6–90 days (relocation, family need, project). | Written end date required |

The 50-mile test uses your current residential address in HarborHub, not the address on your driver’s license if they differ. Update HarborHub within 14 days of a move. A move that changes your tax state or that brings you inside or outside the 50-mile radius must be approved **before** you relocate (Section 9).

Managers cannot reclassify someone as remote to avoid office attendance. Reclassification is a People Operations action.

## 3. Hub employee schedule

Default in-office days for hub employees are **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**. Teams may pick a different three-day pattern if the director publishes it and Workplace can support badge density.

Monday and Friday are the default work-from-home days for hub employees. They are still workdays. PTO is required if you are not working.

Exceptions that do not need a formal accommodation:

- A pre-planned customer on-site, conference, or inter-office trip (code the days as work travel).
- A same-week substitution: you may swap Friday for Tuesday once per month if your manager agrees in Slack and you still hit three office days that week.
- Company holidays and approved leave.

If you will be under three office days in a given week, enter PTO, sick, leave, or travel so Workplace reporting stays honest. Repeated weeks under three days without a code will prompt a conversation with your manager and then People Operations.

New hires in hub roles spend their **first 10 working days** fully in the office (or on a directed travel plan if they are relocating). See **POL-ONB-007**.

## 4. Core hours and meetings

Regardless of category, employees must be reachable during **core hours: 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.** in their local time, Monday through Friday, except during meal time, PTO, or leave.

Cross-hub meetings should be scheduled in the overlap of Seattle (Pacific), Austin (Central), and Dublin (Ireland). A practical band is **8:00–10:00 a.m. Pacific / 4:00–6:00 p.m. Ireland**. Do not make Dublin employees a standing 7:00 p.m. audience. Do not make Seattle employees a standing 6:00 a.m. audience.

Cameras are default-on for interviews, performance conversations, and customer calls. They are optional for large internal all-hands. Managers may not run a “camera always on at your desk” monitoring regime. Harborline does not install productivity spyware. See **POL-SEC-005** for the limited endpoint software we do deploy.

Non-exempt employees must still record meal periods and may not be asked to stay logged into Slack during unpaid time.

## 5. Workspace standards

Whether you sit in a hub or at home, you need:

- A private space where customer audio cannot be overheard by household members or café neighbors when you discuss Confidential or Restricted data.
- A locked screen (5-minute timeout) and the ability to take a confidential call behind a closed door or with a headset in a private room.
- Reliable internet that sustains 20 Mbps down / 5 Mbps up during core hours. Run the Harborline network check in Harborline Learn during week one.
- A work surface and chair that do not cause an immediate injury. Workplace will fund an ergonomic assessment after a reported strain; we do not prescribe a brand of chair.

Public cafés and coworking spaces are allowed for **Internal** and **Public** work only. They are not allowed for Restricted customer data, production access, or payroll. If you must take a Restricted call while traveling, use a headset and do not screenshare production data on a wall-facing laptop.

Children, roommates, and pets will appear in real life. That is fine. Customer credentials on a sticky note behind them are not.

## 6. Stipends and home-office cost

Approved remote employees and hub employees who regularly work from home (everyone in a hybrid pattern) receive:

| Item | Amount | When | How it is paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home office setup | **$500** one-time | Once per employment, after day 30 | Expense in Voyage, category “Home office setup” |
| Internet stipend | **$50 per month** | While in hybrid or remote status | Payroll taxable stipend, not an expense report |
| Coworking (optional) | Up to **$200 per month** | Remote employees only, pre-approved | Voyage, receipts required |

The $500 setup stipend covers a desk, chair, lamp, or noise-cancelling headset that IT did not already ship. It does not cover rent, utilities, or renovations. Receipts are required. Unused stipend does not cash out.

IT-issued equipment (laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, docking station) is provided under **POL-EQP-008** and does not come out of the $500.

Hub employees who live within the 50-mile radius do **not** receive commuting reimbursement. Parking at Seattle and Austin is described in the Workplace guide on HarborHub; Dublin employees use a Leap card benefit listed in **POL-BEN-006**.

## 7. Dependent care and “working while caregiving”

Remote and hybrid work is not a substitute for dependent care during core hours. If a child, elder, or other dependent needs active supervision, use sick time, PTO, or leave. Occasional school closures happen; communicate with your manager and block the calendar. A standing arrangement in which you are the only adult caregiver during core hours is an attendance issue unless People Operations has approved an accommodation or a reduced schedule.

## 8. Coming to a hub if you are remote

Remote employees may visit a hub for planning weeks, onboarding, or customer meetings.

- Harborline books and pays airfare and hotel under **POL-EXP-004** when the Company requests the visit.
- You may visit without a Company request up to **5 days per quarter**. Those voluntary days are on your own travel budget unless your director pre-approves Voyage charges.
- Badge access is requested 5 business days in advance through Workplace.
- Visits longer than 10 consecutive business days in a hub city can create a tax presence. People Operations must approve anything longer.

## 9. Moving, other states, and other countries

**Domestic moves (US).** Email hr@harborline.example 30 days before you move if the new address is in a different state or changes your hub/remote category. Harborline currently maintains payroll in Washington, Texas, California, Colorado, Illinois, New York, Georgia, and Oregon, plus a Delaware entity. A move to a state not on that list may be denied because we are not registered to withhold tax there. Do not assume you can “just keep working” from a new state.

**Canada.** Remote — US/Canada is limited to provinces where we have a contractor-of-record or entity arrangement (currently Ontario and British Columbia). Canadian employees receive a written addendum.

**International work.** Working from a country other than your payroll country for more than **10 business days in a rolling 90-day period** requires written approval from the Chief People Officer and the Head of Information Security. Tourist-visa “workcations” are not automatically approved. Risks include permanent establishment, immigration, and export of customer data.

Dublin employees working from the US (or US employees working from Ireland) for a short customer trip are covered as business travel under **POL-EXP-004**, not as a relocation.

## 10. Weather, transit, and office closures

If Seattle, Austin, or Dublin leadership closes an office for weather, protest, or building failure, hub employees default to remote work that day unless the power or network at home is also down. Office-closure days are **worked remotely**, not automatic PTO. If you cannot work, use sick time or PTO.

If your home internet fails on a hub day, come to the office if it is open. If your home internet fails on a remote day, tether from a phone, use a backup site, or take PTO. IT can issue a temporary LTE router for recurring failures; email it-help@harborline.example.

## 11. Performance, trust, and ending a remote arrangement

Remote and hybrid status is not a permanent property right. Harborline may require a hub employee to increase in-office days, or require a remote employee to attend a hub for a defined project, when the role changes. We will give at least 30 days’ notice except for security or customer-contract emergencies.

If performance or collaboration problems are tied to availability (missed core hours, unread incident pages, repeated camera-off customer calls after coaching), the manager uses the performance process. Revoking remote status is a last step and needs People Operations.

## 12. Safety and workers’ compensation

Injuries that arise out of work at a home office can be workers’ compensation claims. Report the injury to hr@harborline.example the same day. Harborline may ask for photos of the immediate work area. We do not inspect your entire home. We do not reimburse injuries that occur while doing household tasks.

Keep evacuation paths clear. Do not run network cables across stairs.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Q. I live in Tacoma, 32 miles from the Seattle office. Can I be fully remote?**  
A. Not by default. You are inside the 50-mile radius, so you are Hub — Seattle unless the role was posted remote or People Operations granted an accommodation.

**Q. I live in Tacoma but my team is all in Austin. Do I still come to Seattle three days a week?**  
A. Yes, unless your director and People Operations recode you. The hub is about a workplace, not about sitting next to your manager.

**Q. Does the $50 internet stipend cover my whole household bill?**  
A. No. It is a flat taxable stipend. You do not submit the Comcast invoice each month.

**Q. Can I work from Lisbon for a month?**  
A. Not without the international exception in Section 9. Ten business days is the informal travel ceiling; 30 days is a formal case.

**Q. Are the three office days measured per week or as a monthly average?**  
A. Per week. A month with one 5-day on-site and three fully remote weeks does not meet the rule unless the remote weeks were travel, PTO, or approved temporary remote.

**Q. I am non-exempt and I answered Slack at 9:30 p.m. Do I record that?**  
A. Yes. After-hours work is work. Managers should not create it. See the handbook timekeeping section.
