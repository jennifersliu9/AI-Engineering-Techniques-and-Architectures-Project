# Equipment and IT Assets Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-EQP-008  
**Owner:** IT Operations  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** All employees, interns, and others issued Harborline hardware  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-RMT-003, POL-SEC-005, POL-ONB-007, POL-EXP-004

---

## 1. Purpose

Harborline issues the tools you need to do the job and remains the owner of those tools. This policy covers the standard kit, optional accessories, phones, lost or damaged devices, home-office hardware versus the $500 stipend, and what you must return when you leave.

IT assets are also security assets. A laptop that is “just a few days late” coming back after a resignation is an open door. Read this policy with **POL-SEC-005**.

## 2. Standard kit

Every regular employee receives:

| Item | Standard options | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop | 14-inch MacBook Pro (current Company spec) **or** ThinkPad T14 (Windows) | You choose once at hire; switches later need a business reason |
| Charger | OEM charger | One spare charger on request if you split home/office |
| Headset | Wired USB or approved wireless | Required for customer-facing roles |
| 27-inch monitor | One | Hub employees may use a docked office monitor instead |
| Keyboard and mouse | Standard USB-C / Bluetooth | |
| Docking station | USB-C dock | Issued when you have an external monitor |
| Laptop sleeve | Standard | |

Interns receive a laptop and charger. Monitor and dock are issued if the internship is 12 weeks or longer or if the manager requests them.

Standard kit is **not** deducted from the $500 home-office stipend in **POL-RMT-003**. The stipend is for furniture and non-IT items IT does not stock.

Hub offices also provide shared monitors, conference-room hardware, and loaner chargers at the front desk. Do not take a shared monitor home without an IT ticket.

## 3. How to request, repair, or replace

- New hire: kit is created from the onboarding form (**POL-ONB-007**).
- Add-on or replacement: open an IT ticket in HarborHub or email it-help@harborline.example.
- Broken device: ticket **plus** do not leave a failing disk in a closet. If the device might be compromised, call +1-206-555-0199.

IT target times:

| Request | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Password / MFA unlock | 4 business hours |
| Defective charger or mouse | 2 business days |
| Laptop failure (loaner) | Same business day if you are in a hub; next-day ship if remote |
| Extra monitor (approved) | 10 business days |
| Role-based software from the catalog | 2 business days after manager approval |

Do not buy a replacement laptop on a personal card and expect reimbursement. Unauthorized hardware is not placed on the Company MDM and cannot receive Restricted data.

## 4. Accessories and non-standard gear

IT will consider:

- A second monitor for roles that regularly compare data side by side (finance, support, data, design). Manager approval required.
- An ergonomic keyboard or vertical mouse after a reported strain. People Operations may join the ticket.
- A more powerful laptop (more RAM / GPU) for machine-learning or large local builds. Director approval and a 24-month refresh commitment.

IT will not issue:

- Personal tablets, gaming consoles, or e-readers.
- Home printers (use Drive + a local print shop or office printers).
- Standing desks or chairs (use the $500 stipend or an accommodation through HR).
- Multiple simultaneous laptops except for a documented dual-boot or security-research role.

Cables and dongles under $25 may be expensed in Voyage once per year without an IT ticket. Above $25, ask IT first so we can send a known-good part.

## 5. Mobile phones

Harborline does **not** issue a phone to every employee.

| Situation | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Most employees | **$40 per month** phone stipend on payroll if you enroll your personal phone in the work profile for Slack, MFA, and email |
| On-call engineers, executive team, and roles that page outside core hours | Company phone **or** the $40 stipend, your choice; on-call must have a reliable device |
| You decline both | You may use a personal phone for MFA only, without stipend, if MDM work-profile is still installed |

The stipend is taxable. It does not require a monthly bill upload.

Company phones are MDM-enrolled and can be wiped entirely. Personal phones with a work profile: IT wipes the work profile only.

Lost personal phone: remove Okta sessions from a computer or call IT immediately. You still report the loss as a security event if the phone had work email (**POL-SEC-005**).

## 6. Lost, stolen, and damaged equipment

Report loss or theft **immediately** to +1-206-555-0199 and it-help@harborline.example. File a police report for stolen devices when local authorities will take one; attach it to the ticket.

| Cause | Employee cost |
| --- | --- |
| Wear and tear, manufacturing defect, documented accident with prompt report | $0 |
| Loss or theft with prompt report and no pattern of negligence | $0 (first incident); **$200** deductible on a second incident in 24 months |
| Negligence (left in an unlocked car overnight, checked in airline cargo, loaned to a family member, liquid damage after a prior warning) | **$200** deductible, or repair cost if lower |
| Intentional damage or refusal to return | Full replacement cost and a People Operations case |

Deductibles are taken from paycheck where state law allows, or billed as a Voyage “employee owed” item. We will not deduct below minimum wage for non-exempt staff.

A cracked screen that you reported the same week is usually wear-and-tear. A laptop that appears six months after you left is not.

## 7. Care, travel, and household use

- You may use a Company laptop for incidental personal tasks (banking, news) consistent with **POL-SEC-005**. Family members may not have their own logins on it.
- Do not take a Company laptop on vacation to a country on the restricted-travel list.
- Do not pack the only laptop in checked baggage. Carry it on.
- Keep food and drinks off the keyboard; it is a cliché because it is expensive.
- Pets and toddlers happen. Use a sleeve. Repeated liquid damage is negligence.

Software you install must follow the catalog rules. Games, torrent clients, and unapproved remote-access tools are out.

## 8. Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a **36-month** cycle, or earlier if IT cannot keep the device patched. When you receive a refresh, you return the old device within **14 days**. You may not keep the old laptop as a “lab machine” unless Security creates a managed exception.

Monitors and docks refresh on failure, not on a calendar.

## 9. Returning equipment

Return is required when you resign, are terminated, change to a contractor role that uses different kit, or go on a leave longer than 90 days if IT asks for the device (most parental leaves keep the laptop).

**Timeline:** equipment must be received by IT within **10 calendar days** of the last day worked. IT emails a prepaid label to remote employees on or before the last day. Hub employees drop off at the office IT locker.

Return set:

- Laptop and charger
- Monitor, dock, keyboard, mouse if issued
- Company phone, badge, keys, security keys (YubiKey)
- Any customer hardware you were holding

Do not wipe the laptop yourself. IT needs to confirm encryption and revoke keys. You may sign out of personal browser profiles.

Final pay may be delayed only as allowed by law if items are outstanding; more commonly we invoice for unreturned items and treat refusal as a conduct / legal matter. Washington and California have strict final-pay timing — People Operations will not break those statutes to wait on a charger, but you will still owe the charger.

## 10. Accessibility and accommodations

If standard kit does not work for a disability, pregnancy, or documented medical need, email hr@harborline.example and it-help@harborline.example together. Examples we have issued: larger fonts and OS magnification as default, alternative pointing devices, captioning hardware, a second monitor as an accommodation (no extra director approval). Accommodation gear is Company property unless People Operations says otherwise in writing.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can I choose a 16-inch MacBook because I like a bigger screen?**  
A. Not as a preference. A 16-inch or more RAM is a role-based exception.

**Q. Who pays if I lose the charger in an airport?**  
A. First incident with a prompt report: we ship another. A pattern: $200 deductible may apply.

**Q. May I buy a $180 office chair on the corporate card?**  
A. No. Use the $500 home-office stipend in Voyage after day 30 (**POL-RMT-003**). Corporate cards are not for furniture.

**Q. Does the $40 phone stipend stack with the $50 internet stipend?**  
A. Yes. They are different lines on payroll.

**Q. I am on a 16-week parental leave. Do I send the laptop back?**  
A. Usually no. Keep it, keep it patched, and do not loan it. IT will contact you if a refresh or a security event requires a return.

**Q. Can I keep my YubiKey as a souvenir?**  
A. No. Hardware security keys are inventoried and reused or destroyed.
