# Workplace Conduct and Ethics Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-CON-010  
**Owner:** People Operations and Legal  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Employees, interns, and (for harassment, safety, and confidentiality) contractors and visitors  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-SEC-005, POL-EXP-004, POL-RMT-003, POL-ONB-007

---

## 1. Purpose

Harborline’s products only work if customers trust us with operational data and if employees can do their jobs without harassment or pressure to cheat. This policy covers respectful behavior, harassment and retaliation, conflicts of interest, gifts, social media, political activity, substance use, attendance, and how we investigate concerns.

It is not a complete list of every way someone can get fired. US employment is at-will (**POL-HB-000**). Dublin employees should also read their contracts. When this policy and a statute conflict, the statute wins.

## 2. Respectful workplace

You are expected to be direct, civil, and professional with colleagues, customers, vendors, and candidates. That includes Slack, email, video, tickets, and in-person rooms.

Harborline does not require a single personality. We do require that disagreement stay about the work. The following are not “just being honest”:

- Slurs, mockery of accent or appearance, or “jokes” about protected characteristics
- Sustained contempt (eye-rolling campaigns, pile-ons in public Slack, excluding someone from the meetings they need)
- Romantic or sexual comments about a coworker’s body or dating life
- Threats, intimidation, or throwing objects (yes, this has happened in other companies; it is not a joke here)

Feedback belongs in 1:1s, review documents, and calmly written tickets — not in a meme channel at someone’s expense.

## 3. Harassment, discrimination, and sexual harassment

Harborline prohibits harassment and discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other protected status.

Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature when:

- Submission is a condition of employment or used as a basis for decisions; or
- The conduct creates a hostile work environment for a reasonable person.

Examples: comments about someone’s body, repeated date requests after a no, displaying sexual images on a Company screen, or a manager implying that travel-room arrangements are “part of being on the team.”

Harassment can come from a peer, a manager, a customer, or a vendor. Customer harassment is still harassment. We will not tell you to “just take it” because the account is large. We may change staffing, escalate to the customer’s leadership, or end the meeting.

This section applies at offsites, conferences, Slack DMs, and home-office video calls. Hybrid work is still work.

## 4. Romantic and family relationships at work

Adult consensual relationships between peers are not banned. You must:

- Disclose to People Operations if you enter a romantic relationship with someone in your reporting chain, or if you would be in a position to influence their compensation, performance rating, or assignment.
- Recuse yourself from hiring, rating, or paying that person.
- Stop any workplace pursuit when the other person says no, even once, even “kindly.”

People Operations will reconfigure reporting if needed. Failure to disclose a reporting-line relationship is a stand-alone violation.

Do not hire or directly manage a family member without Chief People Officer approval.

## 5. Reporting paths (no wrong door)

You can report harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety issues, fraud, or other policy concerns through any of these:

| Channel | How | Anonymous? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your manager | Conversation or email | No |
| Another manager you trust | Conversation or email | No |
| People Operations | hr@harborline.example | No, but limited distribution |
| Ethics hotline | +1-800-555-0148 or the web form in HarborHub | Yes, if you choose |
| Legal | legal@harborline.example | No |

You do **not** have to start with your manager, especially if your manager is the problem.

If you believe someone is in immediate physical danger, call emergency services first, then Workplace or Security.

## 6. Investigations and confidentiality

People Operations (or an outside investigator) will:

1. Acknowledge a non-anonymous report within **2 business days**.
2. Decide whether a formal investigation is needed.
3. Interview the reporter, the respondent, and relevant witnesses.
4. Review Slack, email, badge, or expense records when they are relevant. Company systems are not private (**POL-SEC-005**).
5. Reach a finding and tell the reporter and respondent that the matter was addressed. We do not always share the other person’s discipline.

Participants must not discuss the investigation in team Slack. You may talk to a spouse, a counselor, a lawyer, or a government agency. Coaching someone to “get their story straight” is obstruction.

Harborline uses a preponderance-of-the-evidence standard (more likely than not). Investigations are not criminal trials.

Time: many cases close in 2–4 weeks. Complex ones take longer. We will not leave you in silence for a month without a status note.

## 7. Retaliation

Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is a separate and serious violation. Retaliation includes firing, cutting hours, sudden “performance” paper trails that start the day after a report, exclusion from meetings, or encouraging others to freeze someone out.

Good-faith reports that are not substantiated are still protected. Knowingly false reports are not protected.

## 8. Conflicts of interest

A conflict exists when your personal interests could interfere with Harborline’s interests or with a customer’s interests. Disclose in HarborHub (Conflicts form) or to legal@harborline.example when you:

- Own more than 1% of, or sit on the board of, a customer, competitor, or vendor
- Are in a position to award work to a company that employs a family member
- Have an outside job that uses Harborline time, equipment, or confidential information
- Invent something related to Harborline’s business that you want to pursue outside (read your confidentiality / IP agreement)

Outside employment is allowed if it does not compete, does not use Restricted data, and does not happen during core hours you are paid by Harborline. On-call engineers need manager approval for any second job that could collide with a page.

## 9. Gifts, entertainment, and anti-bribery

Harborline employees may not offer or accept a bribe. Facilitation payments are banned.

**Receiving from vendors or partners**

| Value | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Promotional items under **$25** (mug, t-shirt) | Keep; no log |
| Meals or gifts **$25–$75** | Allowed if occasional; do not let one vendor fund your social life |
| Over **$75** | Disclose in the HarborHub gifts log within 10 days. Decline cash and most gift cards |
| Over **$250** | Decline unless Legal approves (for example, a conference prize you cannot reasonably refuse) |

**Giving to customers** follows **POL-EXP-004**: $75 per person per year for gifts; $150 per-person meal cap unless a sales VP pre-approves. Government and public-sector customers: follow the stricter of this policy and their ethics rules.

Cash, cash-like gift cards above $25, and anything given to influence a procurement are prohibited.

## 10. Confidentiality and speaking for the Company

Customer data, unpublished financials, unreleased product plans, and employee personal data stay inside Harborline systems. That duty survives your last day.

You are not a spokesperson unless Communications says so. Do not give a journalist a “background” quote about an outage or a layoff. Route media to press@harborline.example.

Social media:

- You may identify Harborline as your employer.
- You may not post Restricted or Confidential material, including screenshots of customer tenants or internal Slack arguments.
- If you post about the Company, a reasonable reader should not think you are an official account.
- Harassment of coworkers on social media is still workplace conduct.

Personal political activity is your right. Do not use Harborline letterhead, Slack announcements, or customer lists for campaigns. Do not pressure reports to donate or to display a candidate.

## 11. Alcohol, drugs, and weapons

Alcohol may appear at Company-hosted events. You are responsible for staying professional and for getting home safely. Expense alcohol only as **POL-EXP-004** allows. Intoxication at work, at a customer site, or while on-call is a serious violation.

Illegal drugs are prohibited on Company property and at Company events. Legal cannabis is still incompatible with safety-sensitive or customer-site work when you are impaired. We follow applicable law on testing (reasonable suspicion, post-incident); we do not run random testing for office roles unless a customer contract lawfully requires it for a specific site.

Firearms and other weapons are prohibited in Harborline offices and at Company events, including in bags, except for sworn law-enforcement officers in the performance of duty. This applies even where a state permit would otherwise allow a weapon in a parking area to the extent the law lets us restrict office interiors. If local law requires a parking-lot exception, Workplace will post it.

## 12. Attendance, honesty, and progressive discipline

Be where you said you would be — in a hub, on Slack during core hours, or on PTO. Patterns of no-call / no-show, falsified time, or invented expenses are honesty issues, not “time-off issues.”

Progressive discipline is typical but not promised: coaching → written warning → final warning → termination. We may skip steps for theft, violence, harassment, data exfiltration, or serious safety breaches.

Investigations may include paid administrative leave. That leave is not a finding of guilt.

## 13. Workplace safety and emergencies

Follow Workplace posted rules: evacuation maps, earthquake/fire procedures, and visitor sign-in. Report hazards to workplace@harborline.example.

Seattle, Austin, and Dublin each have a floor warden list. Remote employees should have a household plan; we cannot evacuate your apartment for you.

If you experience domestic violence or stalking that may follow you to work, People Operations and Workplace can help with badge rules, schedule changes, and referrals. You will not be punished for asking.

## 14. Frequently asked questions

**Q. A vendor sent a $100 gift card after a renewal. Can I keep it?**  
A. Gift cards above $25 should be declined or turned over to People Operations for a raffle or return. Log it either way.

**Q. My customer made a sexual comment on a Zoom call. Do I have to keep taking their meetings?**  
A. Report it. We will not require you to absorb harassment as a cost of the account.

**Q. Can I post a photo of our office happy hour?**  
A. Yes if no Confidential whiteboards are visible and colleagues who are clearly identifiable would reasonably expect the photo (public event vs. a small private gathering). When unsure, ask them.

**Q. Is the hotline really anonymous?**  
A. The vendor assigns a case number. Harborline sees the content, not your phone number, if you chose anonymous. We cannot promise that unique facts will not identify you; we can promise we do not hunt for the reporter to punish them.

**Q. Does “at-will” mean this policy is meaningless?**  
A. No. At-will describes duration of employment in the US. We still investigate, we still follow anti-retaliation law, and we still expect managers to apply rules consistently.
