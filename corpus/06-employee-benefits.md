## 1. Purpose

Harborline’s benefits package is meant to be competitive for a company of our size in Seattle, Austin, and Dublin. This policy describes medical, dental, vision, retirement, spending accounts, life and disability, equity purchase, wellness, and employee assistance. It does not list every Summary Plan Description (SPD) clause. The SPD and the insurance certificate control if this policy and the plan disagree.

Enrollment mechanics (windows, qualifying life events, default coverage) are in `benefits-enrollment-guide.html`. Read that file when you start, get married, have a child, or lose other coverage.

Dependents you may add: legal spouse, registered domestic partner where the plan and state allow, and children to age 26 (US medical). You will be asked for a marriage certificate, birth certificate, or similar proof within 30 days of adding a dependent.

## 3. US medical, dental, and vision

Harborline offers two US medical options through our 2026 carrier (announced each October in HarborHub):

- **PPO** — broader network, higher paycheck deduction.
- **HDHP + HSA** — lower paycheck deduction, high deductible, Company HSA seed.

Harborline pays **80% of the employee-only premium** and **60% of the incremental premium** for spouse and children. You pay the rest by pre-tax payroll deduction (US Section 125).

Illustrative 2026 monthly **employee** cost (your HarborHub election screen is official; these numbers are for planning questions):

| Coverage tier | PPO employee share | HDHP employee share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee only | $145 | $70 |
| Employee + spouse | $310 | $165 |
| Employee + children | $280 | $150 |
| Family | $445 | $240 |

Dental and vision are separate elections. Harborline pays 80% of employee-only dental and vision; dependents are 50% Company / 50% employee.

In-network preventive care (annual physical, many screenings, pediatric well visits) is covered at 100% on both medical options. That does not mean every specialist visit is free. Read the SPD.

Prescription drugs use the carrier formulary. Mail-order (90-day) is cheaper for maintenance medication. The EAP (Section 8) can help you find a therapist in-network.

## 4. Health savings and flexible spending

**HSA (with HDHP only).** For 2026 Harborline seeds **$1,000** (employee-only) or **$1,500** (any dependent tier) into your HSA in January, or prorated on the next payroll if you start mid-year. You may contribute additional pre-tax amounts up to the IRS maximum minus the Company seed. HSA funds are yours if you leave.

**Health FSA (PPO enrollees).** Up to the IRS limit. Use-it-or-lose-it with the plan’s grace period as described in the enrollment guide. You cannot have a general-purpose health FSA and an HSA in the same year.

**Dependent care FSA.** For qualifying childcare or adult day care while you work. Annual IRS cap applies. Harborline does not seed this account.

**Commuter.** Seattle and Austin hub employees may set aside pre-tax transit and parking up to IRS monthly caps. Dublin employees use the TaxSaver / Leap arrangement in Section 11.

## 5. Retirement — US 401(k)

Harborline’s 401(k) is available on the first payroll after hire. There is no waiting period.

- **Match:** 100% of the first **4%** of eligible pay you defer. Example: if you contribute 4% or more, Harborline contributes 4%.
- **Vesting:** **Immediate** on both your deferrals and the Company match.
- **Safe harbor / eligibility:** Regular employees 21 and older. Interns are not eligible.
- **Loans:** One loan at a time, minimum $1,000, following the plan document.
- **Roth and pre-tax:** Both available.
- **Auto-enroll:** New hires are auto-enrolled at **3%** pre-tax on day 30 unless they opt out or pick another rate in HarborHub. Increase the rate yourself if you want the full 4% match.

Harborline does not offer a pension in the US. Do not confuse the 4% match with “Harborline contributes 4% whether or not I do.” If you contribute 0%, the match is $0.

## 6. Life, AD&D, and disability (US)

- **Basic life and AD&D:** 1× base salary, Company-paid, minimum $50,000, maximum $300,000. You name a beneficiary in HarborHub. If you name no one, the plan document’s default order applies.
- **Supplemental life:** Employee-paid, evidence of insurability above the guarantee-issue amount.
- **Short-term disability (STD):** Company-paid. After a 7-calendar-day waiting period, STD pays **60%** of base pay for up to 12 weeks. Company medical leave may top this up; see **POL-LOA-009**.
- **Long-term disability (LTD):** Company-paid. After 90 days, LTD pays **60%** of base pay, subject to the policy cap, while you remain disabled under the insurer’s definition.

Parental leave is a separate Company program at 100% pay (**POL-FAM-011**). Do not file STD for bonding leave unless Benefits tells you to for a medical complication.

## 7. Equity and the employee stock purchase plan

Eligible employees may participate in the Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP):

- Lookback and 15% discount as described in the plan prospectus (the legal document wins).
- Contribution 1–15% of eligible pay.
- Two offering periods per year (approximately May–October and November–April).
- You must be employed on the purchase date. Leaving mid-period usually returns contributions without a purchase; see the prospectus.

Incentive stock options or RSUs granted in an offer letter are **not** this ESPP. Those grants have their own award agreements. Questions: stock@harborline.example.

## 8. Employee assistance and wellness

**EAP.** Confidential counseling (typically 5–8 sessions per issue per year), financial consults, and legal referrals. The EAP phone number is in HarborHub and on the back of your medical card. Using the EAP does not notify your manager. Using the EAP does not create a leave of absence; if you need leave, still contact hr@harborline.example.

**Wellness stipend.** Regular employees receive **$400 per calendar year** for gym memberships, fitness classes, home fitness equipment, meditation apps, or a bicycle commuter purchase. Submit receipts in Voyage, category “Wellness.” Unused stipend does not roll. Wearable hardware over $200 needs an itemized receipt and cannot be a general-purpose tablet.

**Family forming.** Harborline provides a lifetime **$5,000** medical-plan-adjacent stipend for adoption, surrogacy, or fertility treatment not fully covered by medical, administered by Benefits. Parental leave itself is in **POL-FAM-011**.

## 9. Time-off benefits that are not “benefits elections”

These are funded as pay practices, not as insurance elections:

- PTO and sick time — **POL-PTO-001**
- Holidays and floating holidays — **POL-HOL-002**
- Medical, bereavement, jury, military, sabbatical — **POL-LOA-009**
- Parental and caregiver leave — **POL-FAM-011**

You do not “enroll” in PTO. You do enroll in medical.

## 10. Workers’ compensation and statutory benefits

Harborline carries workers’ compensation in every US state where we have employees. Report work injuries the same day to hr@harborline.example. Home-office injuries follow **POL-RMT-003**.

Unemployment insurance and state paid-family programs (for example, Washington PFML, California PFL, New York DBL/PFL) interact with Company leave. People Operations will tell you which claim to file so you are not double-paid beyond the 100% Company cap. You must file when we ask; Company pay may be offset by the state benefit.

## 11. Ireland benefits addendum

Dublin employees receive:

- Private health insurance (company-paid employee, employee-paid dependents at the published rate).
- Company contribution to a PRSA of **5%** of eligible pay when the employee contributes at least 5%.
- Death-in-service cover at 4× salary.
- Income protection as described in the Irish employee contract.
- TaxSaver commuter tickets and a Cycle-to-Work scheme.
- Statutory annual leave and public holidays as a floor; Company PTO and holidays are in **POL-PTO-001** and **POL-HOL-002**.

Irish statutory maternity, paternity, and parent’s leave are supplemented by **POL-FAM-011** so that Company pay reaches 100% for the Company-provided weeks. Revenue treatment of benefits-in-kind is the employee’s responsibility to understand; Payroll will apply required deductions.

## 12. What happens when you leave

Medical, dental, and vision usually end on the last day of the month in which your employment ends (US). COBRA packets (US) are mailed by the administrator. You have 60 days to elect COBRA. Harborline does not pay COBRA except as part of a signed separation agreement.

HSA stays yours. FSA is use-it-or-lose-it through the termination date, with limited COBRA for health FSA in some cases.

401(k) can be rolled to an IRA or a new employer plan. Immediate vesting means you keep the match.

ESPP contributions in an open period are refunded if you leave before purchase, per the prospectus.

Equipment returns are **POL-EQP-008**, not this policy. Unused wellness stipend is not paid out.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Q. When does my 401(k) match vest?**  
A. Immediately. There is no four-year vest schedule.

**Q. I started on 12 January. When does medical start, and when must I enroll?**  
A. Medical starts 1 February. You have 30 days from 12 January (until 11 February) to elect. Do not wait for the 1 February start to log into HarborHub; elect as soon as your account is live.

**Q. Does Harborline pay 80% of the family premium?**  
A. No. We pay 80% of the employee-only slice and 60% of the added cost for dependents. Family coverage still has a meaningful employee share (see the table).

**Q. Can I change from PPO to HDHP in June because I feel healthy?**  
A. Only at open enrollment or with a qualifying life event. Feeling healthy is not a QLE.

**Q. Is the $400 wellness stipend taxable?**  
A. Many wellness reimbursements are treated as taxable. Payroll will withhold where required. The $50 remote-internet stipend in **POL-RMT-003** is also taxable.

**Q. I am part-time at 24 hours. Can I put my spouse on medical?**  
A. Default part-time medical is employee-only. Adding a spouse needs Benefits approval and is not guaranteed; email benefits@harborline.example.
