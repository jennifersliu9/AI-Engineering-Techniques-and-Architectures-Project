Rules employees ask about on this page

  • If a US holiday falls on Saturday we observe Friday; if on Sunday we observe Monday. The July 2026 line already applies that rule.
  • PTO that overlaps a holiday is coded Holiday, not PTO. You do not lose PTO hours. See POL-PTO-001.
  • Floating holidays expire 31 December and are not paid out. US employees get two; Irish employees get one.
  • Travelers keep their home payroll calendar. A Dubliner in Seattle on Presidents’ Day is working unless they take leave.
  • Working an observed holiday requires the process in POL-HOL-002 Section 7 (on-call or pre-approved cutover).
