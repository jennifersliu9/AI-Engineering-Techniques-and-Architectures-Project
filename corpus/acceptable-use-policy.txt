--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEMS ARE COMPANY PROPERTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Harborline email, Slack, laptops, badges, cloud accounts, and customer
environments are Company systems. Use them for Harborline work. Light
personal use (news, banking) is allowed. Family logins, torrenting,
and storing a household photo library as the main purpose of the disk
are not.

You should not expect privacy on Company systems equivalent to a
personal device. Security and People Operations may review accounts
during an investigation (POL-CON-010, POL-SEC-005).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- MFA is required on Okta and every internet-facing app.
- Use 1Password for work passwords. Do not reuse passwords. Do not
  put passwords in Slack, email, or a ticket.
- Do not share your login. Managers will not ask you to share a
  password with a new hire (POL-ONB-007).
- If you think a password or MFA device is burned, call
  +1-206-555-0199 and email security@harborline.example the same hour.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Four classes: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.
Customer production data is Restricted even when a field "looks boring."

- Confidential and Restricted data stay on Company systems.
- Do not forward work mail to personal Gmail/Yahoo.
- Do not put customer tenants, credentials, or people data into a
  public AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini, consumer Copilot, etc.).
  Use the approved internal assistant for Internal material.
- USB storage is blocked unless Security approved an exception.
- Exports of Restricted data expire in 30 days. Delete them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Work happens on Harborline-managed laptops (POL-EQP-008). There is no
BYOD laptop program.

- Encryption, 5-minute screen lock, and the EDR agent stay on.
- Personal phones may hold Slack/MFA/email only with a work profile.
- Lost or stolen device: +1-206-555-0199 immediately.
- Cafes are fine for Internal work. They are not fine for Restricted
  data, production admin, or payroll (POL-RMT-003).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PHISHING AND INCIDENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Report suspicious email with the Report Phish button or
phishing@harborline.example. Report suspicious Slack DMs to Security.

An incident (malware, lost laptop, wrong-person email with customer
data, ransomware note) must be reported within ONE HOUR of suspicion:
  phone +1-206-555-0199
  email security@harborline.example

There is no penalty for a good-faith false alarm. Handling it quietly
overnight is not a perk.

Finance will never change a vendor bank account by Slack alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SOFTWARE AND MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Install software from the IT catalog. Unapproved remote-desktop tools,
disk "cleaners," and random browser extensions are out.

Harborline monitors endpoints, VPN, and mail metadata for security.
Illegal content and harassment on Company systems are prohibited
(POL-CON-010).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHEN YOU LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Access ends on the last day. Return the laptop, charger, monitor,
dock, badge, and hardware security keys within 10 calendar days
(POL-EQP-008). Do not wipe the laptop yourself. Confidentiality
continues after your last day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------
By checking the box in Harborline Learn I acknowledge that I have
read this acceptable-use text and that I know the full policy is
POL-SEC-005. I know how to report an incident. I understand that
violations can lead to loss of access, discipline, or termination,
and that contractors can lose their engagement.

Questions: security@harborline.example or it-help@harborline.example.
