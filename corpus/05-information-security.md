## 1. Purpose

Harborline hosts operational data for customers who move freight, schedule technicians, and run wholesale warehouses. A lost laptop, a reused password, or a well-meaning paste into a public AI chatbot can expose that data. This policy states how we classify information, who may access it, what devices are allowed, how incidents are reported, and which consumer tools are banned.

Security is not only an IT function. If you can log in, you have duties under this policy. The short acceptable-use text you sign at hire is a summary; this document is the controlling policy.

## 2. Principles

1. **Least privilege.** You receive the access your current role needs, not the access you might need someday.
2. **Assume the network is hostile.** MFA, encryption, and device health matter on coffee-shop Wi-Fi and on the office LAN.
3. **Report early.** A false-positive ticket is cheaper than a silent breach. There is no penalty for good-faith incident reports.
4. **Customers own their data.** We are a processor. We do not use customer data to train public models, to demo for unrelated prospects, or to debug a side project.

## 3. Data classification

Every file, ticket, and Slack message sits in one of four classes. When unsure, treat the data as the higher class.

| Class | Examples | Allowed locations | Sharing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Marketing site, published blog posts, job ads | Anywhere | Anyone |
| **Internal** | Org charts, office maps, non-sensitive Slack, this policy set | Harborline systems and approved devices | Employees and signed contractors |
| **Confidential** | Employee home addresses, compensation, unpublished roadmaps, non-production customer names in CRM | Harborline Google Drive with named access, HarborHub, Salesforce | Need-to-know; no personal email |
| **Restricted** | Production customer data, credentials, payment data, production logs with payloads, health data, government ID images | Production VPC, approved admin tools, encrypted Company devices | Named access lists; Security approval for exports |

Customer production data is **Restricted** even if a particular field looks harmless. A warehouse address plus a shipment identifier can be enough to harm a customer.

Do not downgrade a class by taking a screenshot and dropping it into Slack #general. Screenshots inherit the source class.

Retention: Public and Internal follow workspace defaults. Confidential people data follows the People Operations retention schedule. Restricted production exports expire in **30 days** unless Security extends them. If you export a CSV to your laptop to debug, delete it when the ticket closes and empty the trash.

## 4. Identity, passwords, and MFA

- Okta is the identity provider. MFA is **mandatory** on Okta and on every app that can be reached from the internet.
- The approved MFA methods are a hardware security key (preferred for administrators) or the Okta Verify / company-approved authenticator app. SMS MFA is allowed only as a temporary recovery method for 7 days.
- 1Password is the Company password manager. Do not store work passwords in a browser’s personal profile, in Apple Notes, or in a spreadsheet.
- Passwords for systems that cannot use SSO must be unique, at least 16 characters, and generated by 1Password.
- Never share a password in Slack, email, or a ticket comment. Use 1Password sharing or a time-limited Okta grant.
- Privileged roles (production SSH, AWS IAM admin, payroll admin, Google Workspace super-admin) require a hardware key and a quarterly access review.

If you think a password or MFA device is compromised, use the Section 9 incident path the same hour. IT can revoke sessions.

## 5. Devices and endpoints

Work happens on Harborline-managed devices described in **POL-EQP-008**. Minimum controls:

- Full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker) enforced by MDM.
- Screen lock at **5 minutes** of idle time.
- OS and browser patches within 14 days of a critical update; MDM will nag, then force.
- CrowdStrike (or the then-current EDR agent) stays installed. Removing it is an incident.
- Local administrator rights are off by default. Request a time-bound exception from IT.

**Personal phones** may install Slack, Okta Verify, Google Calendar, and email through the Company MDM profile. Personal phones may not store Restricted data or connect to production VPN. If you leave Harborline, IT will wipe the work profile only, not your personal photos, provided you enrolled in the work profile correctly.

**Personal laptops and tablets** are not allowed for email, Drive, production, or CRM. There is no BYOD laptop program.

USB storage is blocked on Company laptops except for Security-approved exceptions (a customer air-gapped plant). Use Google Drive or the approved transfer tool.

Lost or stolen devices: call +1-206-555-0199 immediately (24/7) and email security@harborline.example. Do not wait for Monday. Wipe is faster than hope.

## 6. Network, VPN, and remote work

- Hub offices use filtered Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi is for visitors and personal devices; it cannot reach production.
- Remote employees use the Company VPN for production, payroll, and any Restricted system. Internal web apps that are on Okta may not need VPN; the app owner’s runbook says which.
- Home routers should use WPA2 or WPA3. Do not enable UPnP on a network you use for production admin.
- Public Wi-Fi requires VPN for anything except Public browsing. See **POL-RMT-003** for café restrictions on Restricted work.

Do not install “free VPN” consumer apps on a Company laptop. They interfere with EDR and our split-tunnel policy.

## 7. Email, Slack, and phishing

Treat unexpected credential requests, gift-card asks, wire-change requests, and “CEO needs gift cards” messages as hostile.

- Report suspected phishing mail with the **Report Phish** Outlook/Gmail button, or forward to phishing@harborline.example.
- Report suspicious Slack DMs to security@harborline.example. Do not click the link to “see if it is real.”
- Finance will never change a vendor bank account by Slack alone. A second channel (known phone number) is required. See **POL-EXP-004** for payment changes.

Harborline runs periodic phishing simulations. Failing a simulation triggers extra training, not automatic discipline. Repeated failures plus a real click that leads to credential use is a different conversation.

External auto-forwarding of Harborline email to a personal Gmail/Yahoo account is blocked.

## 8. Software, browser extensions, and AI tools

Install software through the IT catalog or a Security-reviewed exception. Do not install random disk-cleaner utilities, torrent clients, or unapproved remote-desktop tools (personal TeamViewer, AnyDesk) on Company devices.

Browser extensions must come from the approved list. Password-stealing lookalikes are common.

**Generative AI and assistants.** Harborline provides an approved internal assistant that does not send prompts to public training sets. Rules:

- You **may** paste Internal material into the approved internal assistant.
- You **may not** paste Confidential people data, Restricted customer data, credentials, or unpublished security findings into ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini, consumer Copilot, or any other public model.
- You **may not** upload customer databases, source from a customer repo, or production logs to a public AI tool.
- Code assistants that run locally or in our tenant (the approved IDE plugin) may see Company source. They may not be pointed at customer private repos without a Security review.
- If a customer contract forbids AI-assisted work, the contract wins. Legal keeps that list.

Shadow-IT AI tools discovered in browser logs will be blocked and the user notified.

## 9. Incident reporting (one hour)

An **incident** includes: malware alerts you do not understand, a lost device, a suspected account takeover, a customer-data email sent to the wrong person, a misconfigured bucket you just found, a vendor who was breached, or a paper file left on a train.

Within **one hour** of suspicion:

1. Call +1-206-555-0199 if the situation is active (account still open, laptop still missing, ransomware note).
2. Email security@harborline.example with what you saw, when, which systems, and whether customers might be affected.
3. Do not power off a possibly infected laptop unless Security tells you to; live memory helps. Do disconnect from Wi-Fi if you see ransomware.
4. Do not discuss the incident in public Slack channels or with customers until Communications and Security agree.

Security will open a ticket, assign a severity (SEV-1 to SEV-4), and tell you what to do next. Customer notification is a Security + Legal decision, not an individual engineer’s decision.

There is no bonus for “handling it yourself” overnight. There is credit for a fast report.

## 10. Physical security and clean desk

- Badge in. Do not tailgate. Visitors sign in with Workplace and wear a visitor badge.
- Confidential paper goes in the locked shred bin, not the recycling tote.
- Whiteboards in glass rooms facing the street are wiped before you leave. Restricted diagrams are not drawn on street-facing glass.
- At home, follow **POL-RMT-003**: lock the screen, control audio, and do not leave a Restricted spreadsheet on a shared family computer — which you should not be using anyway.

Office keys, badges, and fobs are Company property. Report a lost badge the same day so Workplace can deactivate it.

## 11. Vendors and customer access

Vendors who see Confidential or Restricted data need a contract with security terms and, when applicable, a DPA. Do not create a shadow vendor with a personal credit card to process customer data.

Production access for professional-services engineers is time-bound and ticket-scoped. Standing production admin for people who do not operate the service is reviewed quarterly and removed.

Customers who require background checks or specific training will have those requirements in the project kickoff. Completing them is a condition of access, not a suggestion.

## 12. Monitoring and privacy

Harborline monitors Company systems for security and operations. That includes email metadata, endpoint telemetry, VPN logs, and (when investigating) content on Company accounts. You should not expect privacy on Company devices or Company Slack equivalent to a personal diary.

We do not read private messages for curiosity. We do read them when a policy, legal, or security investigation requires it, with People Operations involved.

Personal web browsing on a Company laptop should be minimal. Illegal content, adult content on a shared screen, and torrenting are prohibited.

## 13. Training and access gates

New hires complete the 90-minute security module in Harborline Learn before receiving production or customer access (**POL-ONB-007**). Annual recertification is due each January. Users who are 30 days late lose non-essential access until they finish.

Privileged users complete an extra 45-minute module and a yearly tabletop.

## 14. Exceptions

Only the Head of Information Security (or delegate) can approve exceptions: personal-device production access, USB, SMS MFA beyond 7 days, a public AI use case, or a vendor without a DPA. Exceptions expire. Slack “looks fine to me” is not an exception.

## 15. Frequently asked questions

**Q. I clicked a link and entered my Okta password on a site that looked wrong. What now?**  
A. Call +1-206-555-0199 now. Do not wait to see if anything happens. Security will reset you.

**Q. Can I use my personal Gmail to send myself a file so I can print at home?**  
A. No. Use Drive and print from a Company session, or use the office printer. Personal email is not a transfer tool for Confidential or Restricted files.

**Q. May I put the weekly all-hands deck into a public GenAI tool to make a summary?**  
A. If the deck is Internal and contains no unreleased financials or people data, use the **approved internal** assistant. Do not use a public tool.

**Q. A customer asked me to download their full tenant to my laptop for a workshop.**  
A. Stop. Request a Security-reviewed export path. A full tenant on a laptop is almost never the right design.

**Q. I am a contractor. Does this apply to me?**  
A. Yes, for any Harborline or customer system you can touch. Your agency may have extra rules; the stricter rule applies.

## 16. Enforcement

Violations range from extra training to termination and, for contractors, cancellation of access. Willful exfiltration of customer data will be referred to Legal and, where required, to customers and regulators.
