# New-Hire Onboarding Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-ONB-007  
**Owner:** People Operations — Talent Experience  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** All employees and interns joining Harborline payroll  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-SEC-005, POL-EQP-008, POL-BEN-006, POL-RMT-003, POL-CON-010

---

## 1. Purpose

The first 90 days should make a new hire productive, paid correctly, and safe on our systems. This policy is for the new hire, the hiring manager, the onboarding buddy, IT, and People Operations. It lists what must happen before day one, on day one, in week one, and at 30 / 60 / 90 days.

Onboarding is not optional training you skip because you already used Slack at another company. Access to production and customer systems is gated on the tasks in Section 5.

## 2. Before your first day (preboarding)

After you sign the offer, Talent Experience opens a HarborHub onboarding checklist. Complete it at least **3 business days** before your start date (or as soon as possible if you accepted inside that window).

You will be asked to:

1. Confirm legal name, address, phone, emergency contact, and work location category (**POL-RMT-003**).
2. Upload identity documents so IT can ship a laptop to the right place. US employees will complete Section 1 of Form I-9 on or before day one; do not send passport photos over personal email unless the onboarding portal requests them through its upload control.
3. Choose laptop platform (Mac or Windows) from the standard catalog in **POL-EQP-008**.
4. Submit shirt size only if your team still issues conference clothing; this is optional.
5. Read the handbook acknowledgment so day-one Learn modules unlock faster.

Managers complete a parallel checklist: first-week calendar, buddy name, accounts to request (GitHub, AWS, Salesforce, etc.), and whether the hire is hub or remote. Incomplete manager checklists are the most common reason a laptop arrives on day four.

IT ships equipment to arrive **one business day before** start for remote hires, or stages it at the hub for hub hires. If a customs delay hits Ireland, IT issues a loaner from the Dublin cabinet.

## 3. Day one

**Hub hires** report to their assigned office at 9:00 a.m. local. Bring identity documents for I-9 (US) or the Irish right-to-work originals. Workplace issues a badge. IT images or unboxes the laptop with you.

**Remote hires** join a video welcome at 9:00 a.m. in the time zone on the offer letter (not necessarily Pacific). A People Operations coordinator walks through I-9 video verification where allowed, or schedules an authorized representative. Do not start work before identity verification is complete.

Day-one agenda (typical):

- Welcome and org context (30 minutes)
- Identity and payroll forms (60 minutes)
- Laptop, Okta, MFA, 1Password (60–90 minutes)
- Security module part 1 (can finish on day two)
- Team lunch (hub) or buddy coffee (remote)
- Manager 1:1 to set week-one outcomes

You are paid for day one even if most of it is paperwork. Non-exempt employees still record hours.

US I-9: Section 2 must be completed within **3 business days** of the start date. If documents are delayed, People Operations may allow a start but will terminate employment if the I-9 cannot be completed in the legal window. This is a federal rule, not a Harborline preference.

## 4. The buddy

Every regular hire is assigned a **buddy** outside their direct reporting line when the team is large enough. The buddy is a first-month guide, not a second manager and not a confidential counselor (that is the EAP and HR).

Buddy duties (about 30–45 minutes a week for four weeks):

- Show where policies actually live (this corpus in HarborHub).
- Explain team norms: stand-up time, on-call, how to ask for PTO in practice.
- Introduce 5–8 people the hire should know.
- Check that IT and access tickets are not stuck.

Buddies do not approve expenses, PTO, or security exceptions.

## 5. Week one required training and access gates

Complete these Harborline Learn modules in week one:

| Module | Length | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information security fundamentals | 90 minutes | **Required before production, customer tenant, or payroll access** |
| Acceptable use acknowledgment | 15 minutes | Required for email beyond day one |
| Harassment prevention (US state version as assigned) | 60 minutes | Required within 30 days (California/New York may be stricter) |
| Data classification workshop | 30 minutes | Required before Salesforce or support-tool write access |
| Expense and travel basics | 20 minutes | Required before a corporate card or first Voyage report |

Until the security module is complete, you may use email, Slack, Google Calendar, HarborHub, and the engineering **non-production** playground if IT created one. You may not receive production credentials, customer VPN, or admin rights.

Managers who “share a password so the new person can watch” violate **POL-SEC-005**. Ask IT for a read-only observer role instead.

## 6. Benefits and payroll in the first month

- Direct deposit and tax forms (W-4 / state, or Irish tax documents) are due in week one so the first paycheck is correct.
- Benefits elections are due within **30 days of hire** (**POL-BEN-006**). Put a calendar reminder on day 20. Missing the window means waiting for November or a qualifying life event.
- 401(k) auto-enroll at 3% occurs on day 30 if you do nothing. Change it earlier if you want 4% for the full match.
- Remote / hybrid stipends start after day 30 for the $500 setup expense; the $50 internet stipend starts on the first payroll after your location category is confirmed.

Payroll questions in month one: payroll@harborline.example, not your buddy.

## 7. 30 / 60 / 90-day checkpoints

These meetings are mandatory for regular hires. Interns use a midpoint and an exit instead.

**Day 30 — manager + new hire.** Topics: access still missing, first deliverable, whether the role matches the interview, PTO already planned (encourage it; do not hoard), benefits election status. People Operations sends a survey the same week.

**Day 60 — manager + new hire.** Topics: performance against the 90-day plan, customer exposure, collaboration issues, whether hub office-day habits are forming (**POL-RMT-003**).

**Day 90 — manager + new hire, written summary in HarborHub.** This is not a second interview and it is not automatic termination if something is hard. It is the moment we say the hire is confirmed in role, or we extend the plan by 30 days, or we begin a performance process. Harborline US employment is at-will at all times; the 90-day checkpoint does not create a contractual probation with different firing rules, except where Irish law or a signed contract says otherwise.

Dublin contracts may include a statutory or contractual probation period. Managers in Dublin must follow the contract and Irish procedure, not only this paragraph.

## 8. Immigration, relocation, and background checks

Offers are contingent on a background check appropriate to the role (criminal as allowed by law, education, and employment verification). Customer-facing and finance roles may include credit checks where lawful. You should not resign from a current employer until Talent Acquisition says the check is clear, unless you accept that risk in writing.

Relocation packages, when offered, appear in the offer letter. They are administered by Talent Experience and Finance, not as ad-hoc Voyage claims. Temporary housing more than 60 days needs a tax review.

Work authorization: Harborline sponsors a limited number of US visas. Sponsorship is a specific offer-letter term. Remote work from a country where you do not have the right to work is never allowed.

## 9. Interns and contractors converting to FTE

Interns follow a shorter checklist: I-9 or Irish right-to-work, security module, equipment, and a mentor (not always a buddy). They do not receive 401(k) or full benefits.

Contractors who convert keep completing I-9 as new employees even if they sat in our Slack for a year. Their Harborline start date for PTO tenure is the regular-employee start date, unless the gap was 30 days or less (**POL-PTO-001**).

## 10. Manager responsibilities (summary)

If you hire someone, you own:

- A 90-day plan in writing (one page is enough).
- Calendar holds for 30 / 60 / 90.
- Access tickets submitted 5 business days before start.
- Introduction at the first team meeting.
- No password sharing.
- First two weeks of meetings that are not wall-to-wall so the hire can finish Learn modules.

Managers who consistently skip 90-day write-ups will be coached by People Operations. The quality of onboarding is part of manager performance.

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can I start on a Wednesday?**  
A. Regular US start dates are **Monday**. Exceptions exist for I-9 timing or a holiday week (for example, if Monday is a company holiday, we may start Tuesday). Ireland starts are agreed in the contract.

**Q. Do I come to the office every day in week one if I am a hub hire?**  
A. Yes. The first **10 working days** are in-office for hub roles (**POL-RMT-003**).

**Q. I already took security training at my last company. Can I skip yours?**  
A. No. Customer contracts require Harborline-specific completion records.

**Q. When can I take PTO?**  
A. Accrual starts day one; use generally starts after 30 days unless your offer pre-cleared dates (**POL-PTO-001**).

**Q. Who pays for the taxi from the airport if I relocate?**  
A. Only if a relocation exhibit says so. Otherwise see **POL-EXP-004**.
