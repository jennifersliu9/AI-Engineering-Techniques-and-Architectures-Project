# Parental and Family Care Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-FAM-011  
**Owner:** People Operations — Leave Administration  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Last revised:** 1 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Regular employees  
**Related:** POL-HB-000, POL-PTO-001, POL-HOL-002, POL-BEN-006, POL-LOA-009, POL-EQP-008

## 1. Purpose

Harborline provides paid parental leave so employees can recover from childbirth when applicable, bond with a new child, and return without emptying a PTO bank first. This policy also covers adoption and foster placement, pregnancy accommodations, lactation, and a shorter caregiver leave for serious family illness.

Read **POL-LOA-009** for FMLA mechanics, medical leave that is not parental, bereavement, and disability top-up. Parental leave is the 100% Company-pay program. Short-term disability is the 60% insurance program for a parent’s own medical recovery when that is the better fit; Benefits will tell you if you must file both.

## 2. Definitions

**Primary caregiver.** The parent who will take the longer continuous block to provide day-to-day care after birth or placement. There is no requirement that the primary caregiver be the birth parent. Two Harborline employees who share a child may not both claim primary in the same event; they choose.

**Secondary caregiver.** The other parent, or a spouse/partner who is not taking the primary block.

**Child.** A newborn, a child newly placed for adoption, or a child newly placed in foster care with the intent of bonding (not a short respite weekend). Stillbirth after 20 weeks and pregnancy loss may qualify for medical leave and for a portion of parental leave as described in Section 6.

**Family member** for caregiver leave (Section 8): spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, or a person who lives in your household and depends on you for care.

## 3. Paid parental leave amounts

| Role | Company-paid weeks at **100% base pay** | When the weeks must be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | **16 weeks** | Within **12 months** of birth or placement |
| Secondary caregiver | **8 weeks** | Within **12 months** of birth or placement |

Base pay means regular salary or regular scheduled hourly wages. It does not include bonuses, commissions, overtime, or unearned variable pay. Commissioned employees are paid parental leave using the same average-earnings method Payroll uses for disability (typically the prior two full quarters). Ask payroll@harborline.example for a projection.

Parental leave is available to regular full-time and part-time employees who have been employed **90 days** at the start of leave. Employees under 90 days may still have FMLA (if eligible) or unpaid time; Company pay starts when they hit 90 days if they are still on a qualifying parental leave, for the unused Company weeks.

Interns receive statutory rights only, not the 16/8 Company program.

## 4. How the weeks may be scheduled

- Primary leave is usually one continuous block. You may split it into **two blocks** of at least 4 weeks each if your manager and Leave Administration agree (for example, 12 weeks at birth and 4 weeks when a partner returns to work).
- Secondary leave may be taken as one block or as two blocks of at least 2 weeks.
- Intermittent bonding leave (scattered days) is not the Company-pay default. FMLA intermittent bonding is legally possible in some cases; Company 100% pay applies to the scheduled blocks we approve, not to random Fridays unless we agree in the leave letter.
- You may add unused PTO before or after the Company block. You are **not required** to exhaust PTO first.
- Company holidays that fall inside a paid parental block are not “made up” later (**POL-HOL-002**).

Both parents employed at Harborline: each receives their own entitlement. They may overlap (both home the first 8 weeks) or stagger.

## 5. Coordination with FMLA, state PFML, and disability

Company parental leave **runs concurrently** with FMLA bonding leave and with state paid-family leave. You will usually:

1. Apply for FMLA (US) through People Operations.
2. Apply for state PFML / PFL when we tell you your work state requires or offers it.
3. If you gave birth and the disability carrier covers recovery, file STD for the medical portion. STD at 60% plus Company parental pay is coordinated to **100%**, not 160%.

Failure to file a state claim when instructed can delay Company pay because we need the offset numbers.

Job protection during the Company-paid weeks is at least as strong as FMLA when you are FMLA-eligible. If you are not FMLA-eligible, Harborline still holds your role for the Company-paid parental weeks except in extraordinary restructures that would have eliminated the role anyway.

## 6. Adoption, foster, surrogacy, stillbirth, and loss

- Adoption and foster placement: the same 16 / 8 weeks from the date the child is placed in your home (or the date you are required to travel to receive the child, if earlier, for up to 2 extra unpaid travel weeks).
- Surrogacy: the intended parents are the caregivers under this policy; the weeks start at birth or placement as the legal documents specify.
- Stillbirth after 20 weeks or neonatal death: the birth parent receives Company medical/parental pay for **8 weeks**; the other parent receives **2 weeks**. Additional medical leave may apply. EAP is available immediately. This is in addition to bereavement if bereavement also applies — People Operations will not make you choose in the first week; we will document cleanly afterward.
- Pregnancy loss before 20 weeks: medical leave and sick time as needed; 3 paid days of bereavement-equivalent leave upon request, plus EAP.

The $5,000 family-forming stipend is in **POL-BEN-006** and is separate from leave pay.

## 7. Pregnancy, lactation, and return-to-work

Pregnant employees may request reasonable accommodations (more breaks, a chair, remote days above the hub default, lifting limits, schedule changes). Email hr@harborline.example. We do not require you to begin parental leave earlier than you and your provider decide, unless a safety issue cannot be accommodated.

**Lactation.** For 12 months after birth (longer if a state law says so), Harborline will provide reasonable break time and a private space that is not a bathroom.

- Seattle and Austin: bookable lactation rooms on each office floor we occupy.
- Dublin: a lockable room; ask Workplace if you cannot find it.
- Remote: you control your space; managers must not schedule over a posted pumping block without an alternative.
- A **$250** one-time pump or parts stipend is available in Voyage, category “Lactation,” with a receipt. This does not come out of the $400 wellness stipend.

Return-to-work can include a **2-week** reduced schedule (50–80% hours) at full pay immediately after the parental block, once per event, if the manager can cover the work. Longer reduced schedules are accommodations or part-time role changes, which may change pay.

Keep the laptop during parental leave unless IT requests it (**POL-EQP-008**). You are not expected to Slack. Managers will not “just check in” on product decisions. If you want a volunteer catch-up near the end of leave, you initiate it.

## 8. Paid caregiver leave (serious family illness)

Separate from parental leave, regular employees with 6 months of service may take up to **2 weeks (10 workdays)** of Company-paid caregiver leave in a rolling 12 months to care for a family member (Section 2) with a serious health condition.

- Runs concurrently with FMLA family-care leave when FMLA applies.
- Certification is the same style of form as medical leave.
- This is not for ordinary childcare, school closures, or a cold. Those are sick time or PTO (**POL-PTO-001**, **POL-RMT-003**).
- You may take it continuously or in two blocks.

If you need more than 2 weeks, use FMLA (unpaid or PTO-supplemented), state PFML, or an accommodation.

## 9. How to apply

Email hr@harborline.example as soon as you know, ideally **30 days** before a planned birth or placement, or as soon as practicable after an early arrival or sudden placement.

Include: expected due date or placement date, whether you intend primary or secondary, a rough start date, and whether the other parent is also a Harborline employee.

Leave Administration will reply with a letter stating weeks, pay, FMLA/state steps, and benefits cost-share. Your manager receives dates, not medical details.

Newborns must be added to medical coverage within **30 days** of birth (**POL-BEN-006** and the enrollment guide). Do this even if you are not looking at Slack. A partner or People Operations can walk you through HarborHub if you grant them a ticket to help — we cannot enroll a child without your electronic approval.

## 10. International employees

Dublin employees receive Company parental weeks **in addition to** statutory maternity, paternity, and parent’s leave, coordinated so that total paid time is at least the Company 16 / 8 and at least the Irish statutory entitlements. We do not reduce Irish statutory rights. Payroll will explain which weeks are statutory pay versus Company top-up.

Working from another country during parental leave (a long stay with family abroad) can create tax and immigration issues. Tell Leave Administration before you book. The 10-day international work cap in **POL-RMT-003** is about **working**; unpaid or paid leave spent abroad is a different analysis, but we still need to know.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Q. I am a secondary caregiver. Do I get 8 weeks at 100% or 8 weeks of PTO?**  
A. Eight weeks of Company pay at 100% of base. PTO is optional extra.

**Q. Can two Harborline parents both take 16 weeks?**  
A. No. One primary (16) and one secondary (8). They can overlap.

**Q. Does this apply to adoptive parents?**  
A. Yes. Placement starts the clock.

**Q. I have been here 60 days and my baby arrived early. Do I get paid parental leave?**  
A. Company pay starts on day 91 for remaining unused weeks. Before that, use sick/PTO, FMLA if eligible, and state programs. Talk to us the same week — do not guess.

**Q. Will taking leave hurt my bonus or review?**  
A. Reviews should cover time worked. We do not prorate performance ratings as punishment for protected leave. Some bonuses that are explicitly “days worked” formulas will prorate; the plan document says so.

**Q. Can I work a few hours a week to “stay in the loop” and still receive 100% leave pay?**  
A. Generally no. Working while on Company parental leave can jeopardize disability and state claims. If you want a planned, limited keep-in-touch meeting near the end, put it in the leave letter.

**Q. Is caregiver leave the same as sick-time-for-family?**  
A. No. Sick time covers routine family illness (**POL-PTO-001**). Caregiver leave is for a serious health condition and uses a certification.
