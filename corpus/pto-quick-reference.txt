PTO QUICK REFERENCE — POL-PTO-001-QR

People system: HarborHub > Time Off
Questions: hr@harborline.example

====================================================================
1. WHO EARNS PTO
====================================================================
Regular full-time (30+ hours/week): full accrual below.
Regular part-time (20-29 hours/week): prorated by scheduled hours / 40.
Interns and agency temps: no PTO. They observe office closures.
Ireland: company PTO is ON TOP of statutory annual leave. Combined
time off will not go below 20 working days + public holidays.

PTO accrues from day 1. You may use it after 30 calendar days unless
your offer letter already approved dates (those must be in HarborHub
before you start).

====================================================================
2. ACCRUAL (US / Canada regular employees)
====================================================================
Paid semi-monthly (24 periods). Full-time day = 8 hours.

  Tenure band                         Days/yr   Hours/yr   Hours/period
  Hire through 2nd anniversary           15        120         5.00
  Day after 2nd ann. through 6th ann.    20        160         6.67
  Day after 6th anniversary              25        200         8.33

The higher rate starts on the FIRST pay period that BEGINS after the
anniversary, not on the anniversary date itself.

Example: 24-hour part-time employee in year 1
  120 x (24/40) = 72 hours/year = 3.00 hours per pay period.

There is no unlimited-PTO program. A manager cannot invent a grant.
A higher grant is valid only if it is in the offer letter AND HarborHub.

====================================================================
3. SICK TIME (separate bank, US)
====================================================================
Full-time: 10 days (80 hours) on 1 January, or prorated if you start
mid-year (remaining months / 12).
Part-time: prorated on scheduled hours.
Ireland: follows Irish sick-pay + occupational scheme, NOT this 10-day
US bank stacked on top.
Unused sick time: no carryover, no payout at year-end or termination.
3+ consecutive sick days: manager notifies People Operations (POL-LOA-009).

====================================================================
4. HOW TO REQUEST
====================================================================
HarborHub > Time Off > Request > code PTO (not Sick, Holiday, or Leave).
Minimum increment: 1 hour.

Length of request          Minimum notice         Manager target
1-8 hours                  End of prior workday   1 business day
2-3 consecutive workdays   5 business days        2 business days
4-9 consecutive workdays   15 calendar days       5 business days
10+ consecutive workdays   30 calendar days       10 business days

Tie-breaker for the same week on the same on-call rotation:
earlier HarborHub timestamp, unless a documented customer commitment
requires the opposite. Seniority is not the tie-breaker.

====================================================================
5. BLACKOUTS
====================================================================
Engineering, Customer Success, and Finance may publish blackouts.
  - Announced at least 60 days ahead
  - Max 10 consecutive business days unless CPO approves longer
  - Already-approved PTO stays approved
  - New requests need manager AND function director
Company holidays are never blackout days (POL-HOL-002).

====================================================================
6. CARRYOVER AND YEAR-END
====================================================================
Max carryover into next calendar year: 40 HOURS.
Cutoff: 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time, 31 December.
Exceptions: written People Ops approval before 15 December, OR
state law (California employees: the 40-hour forfeiture does not
apply the same way; ask hr@harborline.example).
HarborHub shows projected forfeiture starting 1 October.
Floating holidays (POL-HOL-002) do NOT carry over.
Sick time does NOT carry over.

====================================================================
7. NEGATIVE BALANCE
====================================================================
Up to 16 hours negative with manager + People Ops approval.
Repaid from future accruals. If you leave while negative, we deduct
from final pay where state law allows (never below minimum wage for
non-exempt employees). Advances larger than 16 hours: not available.

====================================================================
8. HOLIDAYS AND LEAVES
====================================================================
Holiday during PTO = coded HOLIDAY, not PTO. You keep those hours.
Parental leave (POL-FAM-011) is paid by the company; you do not have
to exhaust PTO first.
Jury duty and bereavement have their own codes (POL-LOA-009).
If you start medical/family leave, stop coding PTO for the same days
unless you elect in writing to supplement unpaid leave.

====================================================================
9. PAYOUT WHEN YOU LEAVE
====================================================================
Unused accrued PTO: paid on the final paycheck at current base rate,
except willful misconduct after a documented investigation AND only
where state law allows withholding.
Sick time, floating holidays, and unearned future PTO: not paid out.
Notice: 2 weeks typical; 4 weeks for director+. We may decline new
PTO during a notice period if coverage would fail.

====================================================================
10. QUESTIONS THIS SHEET IS MEANT TO ANSWER
====================================================================
Q: Third year of service, how many days?
A: 20 days (160 hours) starting the pay period after the 2nd
   anniversary.

Q: Can I carry 80 unused hours into January?
A: No. 40-hour cap, unless a written exception or California rules.

Q: Started 15 August, roughly how much PTO on 1 December?
A: About 8 semi-monthly periods x 5.00 hours = 40 hours, minus usage.
   HarborHub is official.

Q: Friday + Monday around a Thursday holiday — do I use 3 PTO days?
A: No. Holiday is Holiday. PTO is only the working days you are away.
